# Draft Feast

A fast-paced card drafting game. Over three rounds, everyone picks one card from
a hand, reveals it, then passes the rest. Score sets of sushi at the end of each
round. Highest total score after three rounds wins.

## Overview

- **Players:** 2–5
- **Rounds:** 3
- **Goal:** Most points after all rounds (including end-game pudding scoring)

## Setup

1. Shuffle the full deck face down.
2. Deal a hand to each player:
   - **2 players:** 10 cards each
   - **3 players:** 9 cards each
   - **4 players:** 8 cards each
   - **5 players:** 7 cards each
3. Begin round 1 drafting.

## Drafting (each round)

Each turn, **all players simultaneously**:

1. Choose one card from their hand (or two if using chopsticks — see below).
2. Reveal the choice and place it face up in front of them (puddings go to a
   separate pudding area; chopsticks stay in play until used).
3. Pass the remaining hand to the player on their **left**.

Repeat until all cards in every hand have been played. Then score the round.

### Chopsticks

If you already have chopsticks in your play area from an earlier turn this
round, you may **use chopsticks** on your turn:

- Pick **two** cards instead of one.
- Return the chopsticks card to the hand you are passing (so the next player
  receives them).

You cannot use chopsticks on the same turn you first take them.

## Scoring (end of each round)

Score cards in each player's play area. Puddings are **not** scored until the
game ends.

| Card | Points |
| --- | --- |
| **Egg nigiri** | 1 |
| **Salmon nigiri** | 2 |
| **Squid nigiri** | 3 |
| **Wasabi** | Triples the value of the **next** nigiri you score this round (3×). Unused wasabi scores 0. |
| **Tempura** | 5 per pair |
| **Sashimi** | 10 per set of three |
| **Dumpling** | 1 / 3 / 6 / 10 / 15 for 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5+ dumplings |
| **Maki rolls** | Most maki icons: 6 · Second most: 3 (see ties below) |

### Maki ties

Compare total maki icons (sum of icons on all maki cards). If tied for most,
split 6 points among tied players. If tied for second, split 3 points among
tied players. With only two players, only first place (6 points) is awarded.

### Pudding (end of game only)

After round 3, compare pudding counts:

- **Most puddings:** +6 each (split if tied)
- **Fewest puddings:** −6 each (split if tied)
- **2 players:** No penalty for fewest puddings

## Between rounds

Clear played cards (not puddings). Deal a fresh hand from the deck for the next
round using the same deal sizes. After round 3, apply pudding scoring and
determine the winner.

## Winning

The player with the **highest total score** wins. Ties share the win.
