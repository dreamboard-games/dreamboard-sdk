# Sketchbook

A two-player deck-builder set in a designer's sketchbook. Each turn you play
ideas from your hand, spend coins on new cards from the studio shelf, and
shuffle your discard back into your deck whenever it runs dry. The first
player to a sketchbook full of masterpieces wins.

## Overview

- Players: 2
- Objective: have the highest victory point (VP) total when the game ends
- Duration: 20–30 minutes

## Components

Each player starts with their own deck of 10 cards (7 Doodles + 3 Ideas) and
draws 5 to a starting hand.

The shared studio shelf has piles of:

- 7 cards each of the 8 kingdom cards (action cards): Brainstorm, Studio,
  Gallery, Open Mic, Critic, Eraser, Sketchpad, Studio Visit.
- 60 Doodles ($1 treasure)
- 40 Sketches ($2 treasure)
- 30 Inkworks ($3 treasure)
- 8 Ideas (1 VP)
- 8 Concepts (3 VP)
- 8 Masterpieces (6 VP)
- 10 Smudges (-1 VP each — gained involuntarily by Critic)

## Setup

1. Each player receives 7 Doodles and 3 Ideas in their personal deck.
2. Each player shuffles their deck.
3. Each player draws 5 cards to their starting hand.
4. Player 1 takes the first turn.

## Gameplay

Each turn has three internal modes that progress in order:

### Action mode

You start the turn with **1 action**. Play an action card from your hand by
spending an action; the card's effect resolves, and you may use any
"+actions", "+cards", "+buys", "+coins" bonuses it grants. Continue playing
actions until you run out of actions or choose to move on.

### Buy mode

You always have **1 buy** by default. Play any treasures from your hand to
generate coins, then spend coins + buys to gain cards from the studio
shelf. Each gained card goes to your discard pile.

### Cleanup mode

Move every card in your hand and in-play area to your discard pile, then
draw 5 new cards. If your deck runs out, shuffle your discard pile to make
a new deck. Pass to your opponent.

## Card actions

| Card | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Brainstorm** | +3 cards |
| **Studio** | +1 card, +2 actions |
| **Gallery** | +1 card, +1 action, +1 buy, +$1 |
| **Open Mic** | +2 actions, +1 buy, +$2 |
| **Critic** | +2 cards. Each opponent gains a Smudge. |
| **Eraser** | Trash up to 4 cards from your hand. |
| **Sketchpad** | +1 action. Discard any number of cards, then draw that many. |
| **Studio Visit** | Gain a card from the shelf costing up to $4. |

## Scoring

| Card | VP |
| --- | --- |
| Idea | 1 |
| Concept | 3 |
| Masterpiece | 6 |
| Smudge | -1 |

Score = sum of VP across every card in your deck, hand, in-play area, and
discard pile.

## Winning conditions

The game ends immediately at the end of any turn when **either**:

- The Masterpiece pile is empty, OR
- Any 3 piles are empty.

The player with the highest VP wins. Ties go to the player with fewer turns
taken.
